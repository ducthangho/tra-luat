WP710: Add a bounded Windows NLM credential refresh and opt-in keepalive with a
real notebook canary, serialized stage, machine-readable failures, and offline
behavioral tests. `nlm-auth-stage`, `secrets-set --nlm` and `secrets-stage` share
one gate: they upload NLM credentials only after a live canary returns the JSON
body of the expected notebook (matching id plus title or name), and read the
credential jar only after that canary; `--force` cannot bypass it. The wrapper
bounds pipe reads by its timeout, makes a new lock owner wait for a surviving
child of a killed owner, classifies failures only from anchored phrases
(otherwise `inconclusive`), and records rc and elapsed time for every step.
